## Provenance
This dataset is a derived work built from a public consumer-review corpus
originally collected by Xiang Zhang, Junbo Zhao, and Yann LeCun (2015).
The upstream corpus is commonly known as the "Amazon Review Polarity"
dataset (arXiv:1509.01626) and is mirrored on the Hugging Face Hub.

## Intended Use
Sentiment and intent classification of consumer product reviews for a
commercial retail analytics product.
